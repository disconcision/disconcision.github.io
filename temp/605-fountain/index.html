Room sizes

How the numbers in the ad were worked out.

The floor plans have no dimensions, so the sizes come from the photos, using things of known size as rulers. Each method was cross-checked against another.

Back bedroom, width. The queen IKEA Malm frame is 66 in wide and 83 in long. In the photo from the closet end, the bed sits against the window wall with a walkway beside it. Correcting for perspective with the floorboard lines puts the walkway at about 23 in, so the room is about 89 in (7.4 ft) wide.

Back bedroom, length. The maple floor is standard 2¼ in strip. That's confirmed against the Malm nightstand, which is 15⅜ in deep and covers about seven boards. About 25 boards run from the foot of the bed to the closet opening (about 4¾ ft). Add the bed's 83 in and the room is about 11¾ ft long. The alcove is about 5 ft wide and 1½ ft deep inside.

Middle bedroom. The desk (about 70 in wide) stood against the east wall in a 2023 photo. Measured against it, that wall is about 113 in (9.4 ft) long. The other direction is read from the plan at its overall scale: about 10 ft.

Check against the plan. Across the house, the back bedroom, the wall and the middle bedroom add up to about 17¼ ft, which is what the plan gives at the scale set by the bathtub and the county's square footage. The plan draws the wall between the two rooms about 7 in too far toward the back, which is why it made the back room look narrower.
