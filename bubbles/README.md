# bubbles

An infinite canvas for exploring word-embedding space — click a point, type a
word, and its nearest neighbors ring the circle, angularly clustered by
similarity. Drag circles together to find words close to both; click words to
grow a purple planar cluster (a polyomino of grid cells, shown bottom-left).

## Run

```sh
python3 -m http.server 8741
# open http://localhost:8741
```

(Needs a server — the app fetches `data/vectors.f32`.)

## Controls

- **click empty space** — type a word, spawn a circle
- **drag the ring** — resize; more circumference fits more neighbors
- **drag a periphery word** — pull it away to reveal in-between words
- **drag circles together** — they intersect only if words exist close to both; shared words appear in the overlap
- **click a word** — toggle it in the purple graph (bottom-left polyomino; each word tiles next to a word it's linked to)
- **double-click / shift-click a word** — spawn its own circle
- **right-click a circle** — delete it
- **scroll** pans, **pinch / ctrl+scroll** zooms

## Data

`data/` holds the top 100k alphabetic words of GloVe (wiki-gigaword, 100d),
unit-normalized float32, generated by `tools/prepare_data.py` from the
[gensim-data](https://github.com/piskvorky/gensim-data) release
`glove-wiki-gigaword-100` (degenerate low-norm vectors dropped; `dict.bin`
flags words with a system-dictionary stem). Similarity is cosine; neighbor
layout is the angle of each neighbor's vector in the top-2 PCA plane of the
candidate pool.

## Polysemy

A word's vector averages its senses, so "bank" alone knows nothing of what
planes do. Every word is therefore probed through its inflection family
("banked" remembers the maneuver, "milked" the exploitation), taking the max
similarity with inflections slightly discounted. Intersection scoring further
subtracts twice the word's hubness (its average cosine to the common vocab —
computable as a single dot with the vocab mean, since vectors are unit length),
a graded rarity penalty, and a demotion for words with no dictionary stem.
Net effect: bank∩plane yields *chartered, skidded, glide*; bank∩yaw yields
*looped, pivoted*; cow∩bank yields *cayman*; sneeze∩calculus refuses.
