bubbles
  1. click any point and type a word to spawn a circle
  2. words near it in meaning appear around the periphery, loosely clustered — brighter = closer
  3. drag the ring to grow the circle and fit more words; drag a periphery word away from its neighbors to reveal in-between words
  4. drag two circles together — if words exist close to both, they intersect and the shared words appear in the overlap; otherwise they refuse
  5. click any word to add it to the purple graph (bottom-left) — each word tiles a grid cell next to a word it's linked to, planar by construction
scroll to pan  ·  pinch or ctrl+scroll to zoom  ·  drag empty space to pan  ·  double-click (or shift-click) a word to give it its own circle  ·  right-click a circle to delete it  ·  hover a word to see its closeness
