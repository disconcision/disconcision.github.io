About

These are the shell commands Claude Code issued while working on Hazel, a live functional programming environment written in ReasonML, between Dec 27, 2025 – Sep 21, 2026: 100,678 commands out of 201,262 tool calls in the sessions that touched Hazel source. The statistics views are computed over all of them; the Browse view carries a sample of 2,928 calls, stratified by purpose and era and topped up with the most complex commands.

Each command is split into segments on &&, ;, | and newlines, and each segment is given a role by a rule-based parser (search, view, filter, edit, build, test, git, and so on). Python heredocs are parsed with the Python AST into a strip of steps. A shape is a segment with its paths, patterns and numbers removed; a sentence is a command reduced to its sequence of roles. The parser is approximate at the edges: under one percent of segments land in "other".

Commands in cards are truncated to 2,500 characters (7,000 for the most complex), and each card shows at most the first 300 characters of what came back. Session titles are the agent's own one-line summaries. Generated 2026-09-21.
